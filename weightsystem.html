Body Mass Index

In humans, this is a system that is used to determine body weight to height. For our purposes, we will be using it for the very build of the creature so we have a starting point. Pick a range that fits your character the best, and give it your best shot from there. You can always adjust this number later.

BMI Range Build Type Short Description
Less than 18.5 A very light, or very small frame. Twig, String bean
18.5 - 21 Light build. Skinny, but not thin. -
21 - 24.9 Average human build. Not chonky, not skinny. A perfectly fine creature
25 - 27 Dense. Muscular or a larger frame. A heccin' chonker.
27 - 29.9 Heavy build. Either bodybuilder-like or bigger. Hefty Chonk
30 - 34.9 Damn!
35 - 40 Huge, or massive build. Also applicable to bulky species. Megachonk
Greater than 40 Extreme mass or almost gigantic frame. OH LAWD THEY COMIN'!!
