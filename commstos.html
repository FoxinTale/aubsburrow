Foxintale's Terms of Service

  • How do I contact you?

    To contact me, check if we share a Discord server first. It'll be easier to see if you ping me there. Otherwise shoot me a message @foxintale on Discord.
    When sending a DM, I kindly ask to not just say "Hi", or "hello" That is not as likely to get my attention. Pllease fisit nohello.net for why.

  • What's the timeframe, or turnaround time?

    I try to aim for within two (2) weeks, but it may be longer depending on complexity. I do have a full time second shift job in retail that eats into a lot of my time (and sanity), so I do ask for patience if you decide to commission me.

    I DO NOT do last minute / "I forgot I had an event tomorrow" commissions. Please find someone else to do that, if this is the case.

  • What do I need to know, really?

    You will need to provide proof of ownership of any non-free assets or bases you would like me to use.

    When completed, I will work with you to help in uploading the avatar to your end. It won't just be "here's the finished thing, figgur it oot".

    There isn't a whole lot I won't do aside from heavily NSFW content, as long as it does not go against an assets (or species) own TOS. I will be upfront if it is something I am not skilled, or knowledgeable enough to do.

    While I will provide updates throughout, if you are unhappy with the end goal, tell me. I will do what I can to make it right. Understand that I am still learning more advanced things, and the outcome may not be as desired.

  • Payment terms

    Paypal is preferred, cashapp is a no-go for me, and we can discuss other options if needed. If, for whatever reason no payment option works, we will try and figure something out.

    All base (starting) prices are final.

    Payment is accepted up front at the beginning. If you wish to change something, add a new asset I do not own, there may be additional costs, which payment for that will be required before final delivery.

    If you force a chargeback through any platform, without discussing it first, you will be blacklisted, and other creators will be notified.
